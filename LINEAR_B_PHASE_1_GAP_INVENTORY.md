### Category 3: Logographic Ambiguities

#### Dual-Function Signs (Syllabic AND Logographic)

Many Linear B signs function BOTH as syllabograms and logograms depending on context. This creates interpretation ambiguity requiring contextual analysis.

**Examples**:

##### *118 - ka / AMPHORA
- **Syllabic**: /ka/ sound
- **Logographic**: Amphora (storage vessel)
- **Ambiguity**: Context-dependent interpretation
- **Gap**: Formulaic position rules incompletely mapped

##### *120 - ko / GRA (grain)  
- **Syllabic**: /ko/ sound
- **Logographic**: Grain (commodity)
- **Ambiguity**: Administrative vs. phonetic context
- **Gap**: When does syllabic become logographic?

##### *131 - ja / CLOTH
- **Syllabic**: /ja/ sound
- **Logographic**: Cloth/textile
- **Ambiguity**: Textile administrative contexts
