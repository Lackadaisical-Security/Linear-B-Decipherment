**Challenge:** Aligning lunar months with solar year

**Lunar Year:** 12 lunar months = ~354 days
**Solar Year:** ~365 days
**Discrepancy:** ~11 days per year

```
Method: Add extra month periodically
Frequency: ~3 years = 1 intercalary month needed
Pattern: 19-year Metonic cycle (19 solar years = 235 lunar months)

Evidence from later Greece:
  - Metonic cycle used (discovered ~432 BCE but likely older)
  - Intercalary months added as needed
  - Month names sometimes repeated (e.g., "Second Posideon")
```
